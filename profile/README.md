## Why we exist

Open the App Store and scroll the privacy nutrition labels of any ten apps you use. The pattern repeats. **Data Linked to You**: usage, identifiers, location, sometimes contacts and health. **Data Used to Track You**: diagnostics, third-party advertising, audience inference. A University of Oxford study of 12,000 iPhone apps ([Kollnig et al., PoPETs 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.13722)) found that 68% send data to tracking companies the first time they're opened, before you tap anything, and 79% carry at least one tracking library.

Those numbers are the reason SideSwipe Labs exists.

We pick categories where the cloud-first default has done quiet damage to the people who use it, then rebuild the category with privacy as the architecture rather than the disclaimer. Compute runs on the Neural Engine and GPU you already own. Storage lives in the iOS sandbox under `FileProtectionType.complete`. No servers in the path of your data. No analytics by default. No ad networks. No required accounts. Fia has no analytics at all; Pressbook and Graycard keep theirs off until you turn it on, and anonymous when it's on.

The privacy guarantees aren't promises we'd have to keep — they're properties of a system that physically can't violate them.

## Our Commitments

Every product decision either reinforces these or breaks them. There is no middle ground.

- **🔒 Privacy by default** — No ad networks. No required accounts. Analytics is off by default and anonymous when on, and Fia has none. The starting position is that nothing about you leaves your device.
- **🔬 Evidence-based design** — Features touching behavior or wellbeing are grounded in published research and validated through structured user testing.
- **❤️ Human-centered** — Accessibility built from the first commit, not added during a pre-launch sprint. VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, reduced motion, color contrast — all standard.
- **⚡ Craft and quality** — We ship fewer products on purpose. Every interaction reviewed, every animation tuned. Slower output, denser quality.

---

## What we don't do

A studio is also defined by what it refuses.

- **No growth-hacked acquisition loops** — No deceptive paywalls, no five-step cancellation flows, no "are you sure?" guilt screens
- **No surprise paywalls** — Free tier large enough to judge honestly from inside the app
- **No user analytics by default** — Where optional telemetry exists, consent defaults to off and all data is anonymized
- **No time-on-app optimization** — We optimize for finished work. An album exported is a success
